# Privacy Policy — StatusSaver

**Last updated:** October 2026

## The short version

StatusSaver collects **nothing about you**. Everything happens on your phone.
The app shows banner ads (via Google AdMob) — Google may use standard
advertising identifiers as described in
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What the app accesses, and why

- **Accessibility service ("StatusSaver smart button")** — the app's
  engine. It notices which app is in the foreground (package and window
  names only) so it can show its small download button while WhatsApp is
  open, and it draws that button itself as an accessibility overlay. It
  is technically unable to read screen content (window-content access is
  disabled in its configuration): no messages, no names, no text is ever
  read, recorded, or monitored. You switch it on manually in Android
  Settings, and can switch it off anytime.
- **All files access** (Android 11+) — WhatsApp keeps viewed statuses in a
  hidden, app-specific folder (`.Statuses`) that normal gallery permissions
  cannot see. The app reads only these WhatsApp status folders, only to copy
  the statuses you choose to save. Nothing else is read. Access can be
  revoked anytime in Android Settings.
- **Storage** (Android 10 and below) — same purpose, via standard storage
  permissions.
- **Gallery (`Pictures/StatusSaver`)** — where your saved statuses are
  stored: one album ("StatusSaver") holding both photos and videos.
  (Versions up to 1.3 also used `Movies/StatusSaver` for videos; the app
  can still show those older saves in its library.)

## What we do NOT do

- No account, no login, no analytics.
- No data leaves your device. There is no server.
- We do not read your chats, contacts, or anything outside the WhatsApp
  status folders.

## A note on consent

Statuses are someone's personal moments. Save your own, or save with the
sharer's permission.
