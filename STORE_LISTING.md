# StatusSaver — Play Store listing (draft v1)

## App name
StatusSaver — Save WhatsApp Status in One Tap

## Short description (≤80 chars)
Floating SAVE button: download any viewed WhatsApp status without leaving it.

## Full description (EN)
Tired of leaving WhatsApp to save a status? StatusSaver puts a small floating
**⤓ SAVE** button right on your screen. Viewing a status you love? One tap —
it's in your gallery. No switching apps, no hunting through folders.

- One-tap save with the floating button — never leave WhatsApp
- Saves photos and videos to your gallery (Pictures/StatusSaver)
- Clean design, no shady popups
- Works fully on your device — your data never leaves your phone

**How it works:** WhatsApp temporarily keeps viewed statuses on your phone.
StatusSaver copies the one you're viewing into your gallery when you tap SAVE.

**Please respect others:** save your own statuses, or save with permission.
